Hypotheses

A hypothesis is the question a round of reviewing is meant to answer: which claims it concerns, and how many of them have to be reviewed for the answer to hold up. Processing one counts the matching claims in the warehouse and derives a sample size from them. Fetch claims under a hypothesis to have those reviews count towards it. {% if not can_manage %}
Everyone can see hypotheses and review against them; creating and editing them is limited to administrators and reviewers who have been granted it. {% endif %}
